Common Questions from Captains

Got questions about pitch rules, weather, or equipment? Click each question to expand the answer.

We provide a 100% Rain Guarantee. If a match is abandoned due to unplayable rain conditions before 50% of the scheduled overs/time is completed, you receive an instant free full reschedule voucher valid for 90 days across any of our grounds, or a 100% credit refund. Our championship grounds also feature high-efficiency sand-slit sub-surface drainage which can dry out the outfield within 45 minutes of rain stopping.

You only need to pay a 30% advance deposit online via UPI, Credit Card, or Net Banking to confirm and lock your slot on the calendar. The remaining 70% balance is settled at the venue pavilion reception desk prior to the match toss.

Yes, absolutely! You are completely free to bring your own cricket kits, bats, pads, gloves, and balls. Alternatively, if you need extra match balls (Four-piece Red, White, or Pink Kookaburra / SG) or team jerseys, you can easily add them to your booking or purchase them at our onsite Pro-Shop.

Yes! All slots scheduled after 6:00 PM automatically include full stadium-grade 600 Lux floodlights along with instant automatic diesel generator (DG) backup. If the municipal power grid fluctuates, our heavy generators kick in within 3 seconds, ensuring zero disruption to your match.

On The Oval and Lord's Meadow natural turf pitches, full cricket spikes and rubber stud shoes are permitted. On ThunderBox 24/7 Turf and SpeedPro Practice Nets, metal spikes are strictly prohibited to protect the synthetic grass fibres; players must wear flat rubber turf shoes or sports sneakers.

Yes! We have certified BCCI and State Cricket Association panel umpires and trained CricHeroes live scorers available as an add-on. We also provide full wireless integration with our boundary LED electronic scoreboard.

You can reschedule your slot at zero charge up to 48 hours before match day. For cancellations requested at least 72 hours prior to the slot, 100% of your advance deposit is refunded. Within 48 hours, advance deposits can be transferred as credits for a future fixture.

Yes! Both The Oval and Lord's Meadow bookings include complimentary access to 2 full air-conditioned player pavilions with lockers, private washrooms, hot water showers, and tactical whiteboards for both competing squads.
